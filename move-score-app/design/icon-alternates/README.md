# App icon alternates

The app icon is **Rally M** (`icon-01-rally-m.svg`, chosen October 2026). Kept here for later:

- `icon-03-flap-40.*` — Flap 40, the split-flap scoreboard reading 40 (shortlisted; may be reconsidered).
- `icon-00-ball-on-stage.png` — the icon used before Rally M.

To switch, copy the 1024 px PNG to `assets/brand/icon.png` and rebuild `assets/brand/adaptive-icon.png`
(the symbol alone on a transparent 1024 canvas, inside the centre 66%). All concepts and both logo kits:
`showcase/brand/` (gallery: https://claude.ai/artifact/SvQWBZhrTerYcVrXbm1DbM).
